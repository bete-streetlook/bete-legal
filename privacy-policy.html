Privacy Policy

Effective 2 September 2026 · Bete iOS app

Who this covers

This policy describes how the Bete iOS app (“Bete”) handles information on your device. Bete is a local street look archive. It does not operate a Bete account server and does not send your looks, notes, or media to a Bete backend.

What stays on this device

Bete stores the following locally in the app sandbox and, where noted, in Apple system stores:

  • Look media copies you shoot or import, titles, fit notes, reveal times, and archive cards
  • A one-tap local archive id and session token (not an Apple ID, not a password)
  • Style Archive Credit balance and StoreKit purchase records used to prevent double-granting
  • Style circle history, onboarding and appearance preferences

Album originals are not rewritten. A look is copied into Bete’s app folder.

What we do not collect

Bete does not run its own analytics, advertising, or tracking SDK. It does not ask for App Tracking Transparency. It does not upload your archive to a Bete website or cloud folder.

Permissions you tap for

Camera, Photos, and Microphone stay off until you tap the matching action (shoot, import, speak notes, or save a card to Photos). Speech recognition is used only to turn spoken fit notes into text on this device. You can type notes instead.

Purchases

Consumable Style Archive Credits are sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment. Bete records a verified transaction id on this device so credits are granted once. Credits live on this device and cannot be restored after you delete the app, clear local data, or use Delete Account. Apple charges are refunded only through Apple.

Account actions

Log Out clears the local session and returns you to one-tap login. Looks on this device are kept.

Delete Account removes the local archive id and the looks, media copies, style circle history, and purchase ledger written under it on this device. This cannot be undone.

Sharing

Bete does not post to a feed or send live chat. If you use the system share sheet or save a card to Photos, that copy follows Apple’s sharing and Photos rules, not a Bete server.

Children

Bete is not directed at children under 13. Do not use it to store another person’s look without a lawful basis you already have.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change. Continued use after that date means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Use Support inside Bete (Settings → Support). There is no separate web form.
