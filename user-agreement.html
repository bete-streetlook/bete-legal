Terms of Use · Effective 2 September 2026 · Bete iOS app

The app

Bete is a local street look archive. You shoot or import a look, write fit notes, set a reveal time, spend credits, and keep one archive card on this device. There is no public feed and no live chat.

License

Apple’s Licensed Application End User License Agreement applies except where this User Agreement is more specific. You may use Bete for personal, non-commercial archiving of looks you are allowed to record.

Account

Sign-in is one-tap on this device only. There is no Sign in with Apple and no username or password. One-tap creates a local archive id. You must be able to Log Out and to Delete Account from Settings. Log Out keeps looks. Delete Account removes the id and the looks written under it on this device.

Credits

Archiving a card spends Style Archive Credits. Packs are consumable In-App Purchases sold and billed by Apple at the prices shown on the payment sheet. Previewing a look does not spend credits. Credits are not a bank balance, are not transferable, and are not restored after Delete Account, Clear All Local Data, or deleting the app. Request refunds from Apple.

Style circle

Style circle content is scripted on this device. It is not a live person, post, or message sent over a network.

Your content

You are responsible for the looks and notes you store. Do not import media you do not have the right to use. Bete copies selected media into the app folder; it does not claim ownership of your photos.

Acceptable use

Do not use Bete to break the law, to store others’ private images without permission, or to reverse-engineer Apple’s payment system. We may refuse support if you do.

Disclaimer

Bete is provided as-is. Media processing, reveal timers, and local files can fail (for example if a work copy is missing). The app will show a repair or error state instead of pretending a card was written.
